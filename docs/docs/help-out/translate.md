# Translating

You can help to translate Rxtx Share into your language.
On [Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/rxtx-share) you can easily translate Rxtx Share online.

Is your language not on Crowdin? Feel free to [Request it](https://github.com/stonith404/rxtx-share/issues/new?assignees=&labels=language-request&projects=&template=language-request.yml&title=%F0%9F%8C%90+Language+request%3A+%3Clanguage+name+in+english%3E).

Any issues while translating? Feel free to participate in the [Localization discussion](https://github.com/stonith404/rxtx-share/discussions/198).
